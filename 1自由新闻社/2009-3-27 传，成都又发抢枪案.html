                                                                  The Information Center for Human Rights and Democracy quoted its sources as saying the sentry was standing guard at the headquarters of the 149th division of the Chengdu Military Region in Leshan city on Thursday when the incident occurred.

                                                                  After stabbing the soldier in the chest, inflicting only minor injuries, the attacker fled the scene, the Hong Kong-based center said.

                                                                  In that incident, which was reported by state-run media, the sentry was also robbed of his submachine gun.

                                                                  The center said military garrisons in Chongging and Sichuan have stepped up security after the attacks, stacking sandbags and setting up restricted zones, while shot-to-kill orders have been issued against trespassers who ignore three warnings.
